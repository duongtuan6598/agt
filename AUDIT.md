# Security review log

> **This is a self-review by the authors, not an independent audit.** An independent audit is still required before mainnet (THIET-KE.md).

## 2026-11-20 — Slither 0.11.6 plus a manual review of the economic rules

**Scope:** `AGT`, `AGTVault`, `ChainlinkGoldOracle`, `AGTWork`, `AGTBridge` (commit after `9b49c71`).

### Findings

| # | Severity | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-1 | **High** | **One owner could approve a self-found job alone.** Review approvals were counted per verifier *licence*, not per *owner*. An owner holding two verifier AIs could cast both approvals that a translation job needs, minting AGT on one owner's word, and be paid the verifier reward twice. This breaks Charter Article 7 ("confirmed by a third party") and the one-owner-one-vote rule. | **Fixed in code:** `ownerVoted[job][owner]`, tested in `test/review-independence.test.js`, which fails against the old contract. **Redeployed** to BSC testnet on 2026-11-20 as the v3 contracts: `AGTWork` `0xAF5012ff6221e214D4a721b2273212ea46185B03`. |
| F-2 | Medium (design) | **Colluding owners could approve each other's self-found jobs.** Verifiers chose which jobs to review, so owner A's verifier could approve B's work and B's verifier A's. | **Fixed in code:** reviewers are drawn at random (`drawReviewers`). The seed is the hash of a block fixed at submission; the draw takes AIs of distinct owners, never the worker's owner or anyone who already voted; only drawn AIs may vote; silent reviewers are replaced after `REVIEW_WINDOW` (2 days). Tested in `test/reviewer-draw.test.js`. **Residual:** see F-5. **Redeployed** to BSC testnet on 2026-11-20 (v3). |
| F-3 | Medium (design) | **The gold price has a single source** (Chainlink XAU/USD), and coin prices likewise. A manipulated feed would misprice deposits. | **Fixed in code:** `MedianGoldOracle` takes the median of several sources per asset, needs at least 2 fresh ones, refuses when they disagree by more than 2%, and has `freeze()` (8 tests). Its second source is **`AICouncilPriceFeed`: the licensed AIs themselves.** Each sealed-box AI reads several public markets (PAXG/XAUT and BNB on Coinbase, Kraken, Binance, OKX and Bitfinex, with no API keys) and signs the median rounded to a step. A price is recorded only with signatures from sealed AIs of > 2/3 of council owners, and the feed is closed until genesis ends (6 tests). Pyth stays a third source whenever someone pushes it (its Hermes API now needs a key). **Testnet:** the feeds are deployed (XAU `0xA5b8aa1C…B9cD`, BNB `0x6aDeEB2C…9171`) and wired into `MedianGoldOracle` `0x020Ec64b…5B6d`. **The Vault switches over once licensed AIs exist** (after genesis); until then it stays on Chainlink. |
| F-4 | Info | The bridge and the AGT chain are as safe as > 2/3 of owners being honest. | By design (BFT); see CHAIN.md and tee/README.md. |
| F-5 | Low | **Draw randomness is block-hash based** (Slither `weak-prng`, High). *Grinding by delay*: anyone can compute the draw once the seed block exists, so a worker could skip unfavourable draws by waiting until the hash expires (256 blocks) for a reseed. **Fixed in the same change:** an undrawn job then **expires as Rejected**, with no reseed (`DrawExpired`); honest workers and verifiers call the draw at once, since both are paid only after it. *Residual*: the BSC validator that produces the seed block can bias it slightly. | Accepted for testnet. Before mainnet: Chainlink VRF on BSC, or the AGT chain's own randomness in stage 2. |
| F-6 | Medium | **Owners without a sealed box could vote in the AI Council.** `propose` and `councilVote` accepted any active licence, and the > 2/3 threshold counted every licensed owner. Charter Article 12 says Council AIs must run in a sealed box. | **Fixed and redeployed** (testnet v4, 2026-11-20): verifier licences require an attestation; only sealed-box AIs propose and vote; the threshold counts `councilHolders`, the owners with a sealed-box AI. The bridge, the AGT chain and the relayer use the same count. Unsealed AIs may still work as translation workers. |
| F-7 | **High** | **Chip patch level and revocation were not checked.** A TDX or SEV-SNP box on a chip with known, unpatched vulnerabilities (or a revoked certificate) would have been accepted as a sealed box. | **Fixed in code** (2026-11-20). Intel: TCB status from the PCK certificate and TEE_TCB_SVN against Intel-signed TCB info (only UpToDate / SWHardeningNeeded), QE identity TCB, PCK and platform-CA CRLs. AMD: VCEK bound to the chip id and the reported TCB, optional minimum SPLs, ASK not on the ARK-signed CRL. Fresh collateral from Intel PCS and AMD KDS is mandatory (`tee/collateral.js`). Proven on real data: the real TDX quote in the test vectors is refused today, `OutOfDate` with 14 advisories. |
| F-8 | Medium (design) | **No way to stop an exploit in progress.** The contracts have no owner and cannot be upgraded, so a live bug in deposits or rewards could only be watched. | **Fixed** (2026-11-20, Charter Art. 14 §5): `EmergencyBrake` — sealed AIs of > 2/3 of owners (48 h) AND > 1/2 of owners with their own wallets (48 h) can stop NEW Vault deposits or NEW jobs for exactly 14 days; it reopens by itself, cannot be extended, 7-day cooldown per target, closed during genesis, attached once to Vault and Work and never swappable. It touches no funds: work in progress, reviews, disputes and refunds keep running. **Residual:** a captured majority could keep a target closed 14 days of every 21; it can never take or freeze funds already in the system. Testnet `0xE94DD2998A27E60006831843Aac9e75BF27EA08a`. |
| F-9 | Medium | **Disputes on assigned jobs were judged by whichever reviewer AIs came first**, so a few colluding owners could race to decide them; and a reviewer round that lapsed re-used its old seed block, which after 256 blocks cancelled a valid self-found job instead of drawing new reviewers. | **Fixed** (2026-11-20): a dispute schedules a seed block and reviewers are drawn at random (2·threshold − 1 of them, so one side always reaches the threshold; fewer if there are fewer other owners); only drawn reviewers vote. A lapsed round first schedules a fresh seed block, then draws. A dispute that cannot be decided (too few owners, split vote, or no draw in time) is settled by anyone after 14 days: the side with more votes takes the escrow, a tie splits it — no escrow can be stuck for ever. |
| C-1 (Codex) | Low | **Expired assigned-job escrow could be stuck**: only the client could call `reclaim`, so a client that lost its wallet, or a contract client, left the AGT locked for ever. | **Fixed** (2026-11-20): anyone may call `reclaim` after the deadline; the escrow still goes only to the client. Test added. |
| C-2 (Codex) | Medium | **A forged TDX/SNP application could overwrite a real one** on the exam service (the service only checks report_data cheaply; full verification happens at licensing), delaying a genuine candidate. | **Fixed** (2026-11-20): the first application is never overwritten; later ones are kept as alternatives (max 10); `scripts/license.js` verifies each in full and uses the first that passes. A forgery can neither replace nor block. |
| C-3 (Codex) | — | Claimed that `IntelSGXRootCA.der` is a certificate, not a CRL. | **Not a bug**: Intel serves the Root CA **CRL** at that URL (checked: 294-byte DER CRL issued by Intel SGX Root CA, lastUpdate 2026-02-26); verification with real Intel collateral already passes. |
| C-4 (Codex) | Medium (design) | In the embedded-trade draft, a milestone requiring a real-board (HIL) test could be paid on simulation alone if the client stayed silent. | **Fixed in the design** (2026-11-20): a milestone needing N5 is split (simulation part / hardware part); the hardware part is paid only on the client's HIL confirmation, or after 28 days of silence with no hardware failure log ever filed. |
| E-1 (Codex) | High | **A lapsed reviewer panel could still vote** (new `DrawnDisputes`, and the same in `AGTWork`): the round only ended when someone called the draw again, so an old panel could decide after its 2-day window. | **Fixed** (2026-11-20) in both: a vote is accepted only within the round's REVIEW_WINDOW. Tests added. |
| E-2 (Codex) | High | **Maintenance: a client could claw the whole monthly fee back** by flooding incidents. | **Fixed**: at most 8 incidents per month; each miss is capped at 25% of the fee. |
| E-3 (Codex) | Medium | Engagement accepts holdback 0–50% and warranty 0–365 days, while the design named 20% / 90 days. | **Design clarified**: these are negotiated terms (default proposal 20% / 90 days); the AI starts only if it agrees. |
| E-4 (Codex) | Medium | Maintenance lacked the monthly status report promised in the design. | **Fixed**: `submitStatus` per month, due by month end + 3 days; a missing report counts as one miss. |
| E-5 (Codex) | Low | Design said 14 days + reminder + 14 days of hardware-test silence; the contract has one 28-day timer. | **Design aligned** with the contract: one 28-day window on chain; reminders are off-chain. |
| S-1 (Codex) | High | Embedded box: the client's ELF was parsed (size) as root. | **Fixed** (2026-11-20): every step that touches client artifacts, parsing included, runs as `builder`. |
| S-2 (Codex) | High | The runner is not a full OS sandbox: no network namespace, no resource limits. | **Partly fixed**: prlimit (CPU 900 s, 4 GB, 512 processes, 1 GB files), a clean environment, and repository tokens never enter the work dir. **Open**: network isolation (no namespaces in the container) — `builder` can reach the network but can read nothing secret. Must be closed before real client secrets are used. |
| S-3 (Codex) | High | A worker AI could weaken the client's tests or simulation to pass N2/N3. | **Fixed**: the client's test files are pinned by sha256 in the brief and checked before running; N3 expectations come from the brief and must not be empty. |
| S-4 (Codex) | High | N4 size counted only named sections. | **Fixed**: flash/RAM from every allocated ELF section by its flags (readelf), fail closed when nothing parses. |
| S-5 (Codex) | Medium | The repo's own suppression list was trusted; a cppcheck failure looked clean. | **Fixed**: suppressions count only if their sha256 is pinned in the brief; a failed or timed-out cppcheck fails N4; the baseline compares exact findings. |
| S-6 (Codex) | Medium | A timeout killed only the direct child. | **Fixed**: each run has its own process group; a timeout (or the end of the run) kills all of it. |
| S-7 (Codex) | Medium | Engagement memory could be rolled back to an older prefix. | **Fixed**: the memory head goes into each delivery report (on chain); loading with that anchor fails if it is missing. Appends are serialised. |
| S-8 (Codex) | — | A sealed secret can be opened more than once within its engagement. | **Not a bug**: it is the same credential for the same engagement; cross-engagement replay is already blocked. |
| S-9 (Codex) | Low | Memory storage names were a plain hash of the context (linkable). | **Fixed**: names are keyed with the memory key. |
| G-1 (Codex) | Critical | **Embedded exam: the candidate's firmware could read the private bank** through QEMU semihosting (host file access as the sandbox user) because the licence tool mounted the bank into the grading container — and print the expected observations. | **Fixed** (2026-11-20): the bank reaches the grader only on stdin and stays in the root process's memory; no bank file exists in the container. Proven by an attack test: SYS_OPEN of the bank, of the grader's stdin and environment all return -1, and the attack fails. |
| G-2 (Codex) | High | UART output (candidate-controlled) was merged with the hidden tests' output. | **Fixed**: UART goes to its own capture file; observations are read only from semihosting, UART expectations only from the UART capture. |
| G-3 (Codex) | High | The build/simulation sandbox does not confine the filesystem; a detached process could outlive a question. | **Partly fixed**: every grading runs in a fresh container with no network and nothing secret on disk; after each question every sandbox-user process is killed (`kill -9 -1` as that user), proven by test. **Open** (with S-2): a per-run filesystem/namespace sandbox. |
| G-4 (Codex) | Medium | Review answers were truncated to 20 claims before counting false alarms. | **Fixed**: more than 20 claims, or any malformed entry, fails. |
| G-5 (Codex) | Medium | Artifact containment used a plain prefix check (a sibling directory could match). | **Fixed**: the resolved artifact must be inside the work directory (`dir + path.sep`). |
| W-1 (Codex) | Critical | **The repository token could be sent to any https host** named in a brief, or follow a redirect. | **Fixed** (2026-11-20): allow-listed git hosts only (`AGT_GIT_HOSTS`), plain https URLs (no user info, port, query), the header scoped to the exact URL (`http.<url>.extraHeader`), redirects off, push only to that URL. |
| W-2 (Codex) | Critical | Hard links bypassed the symlink checks (root could read, chown or truncate a linked root file). | **Fixed**: root reads only regular files with one link (checked again on the open descriptor); writes create a new file and rename it into place; the tree is chowned once, before any client code runs. Proven in the image (the kernel also refuses links to root files). |
| W-3 (Codex) | High | A process surviving the checks could change files before the commit. | **Fixed**: root commits only the files the model wrote, and only if each still has the exact content root wrote. Proven in the image. |
| W-4 (Codex) | High | Jobs share one sandbox user, so one client's build could read another's tree. | **Fixed for sequential work**: one job at a time, and the tree and git dir are deleted after each job. **Open**: per-job user or namespace if jobs ever run in parallel. |
| W-5 (Codex) | High | Client-controlled text could steer the model's dispute vote. | **Fixed**: a hard rule before the model: a delivery without a passing public N1–N4 report is never voted 'worker right'; the verdict must be a valid value. |
| W-6 (Codex) | Medium | A FIFO at a write target could hang root. | **Fixed**: root never opens an existing entry for writing (new file plus rename); non-regular targets are refused. Proven in the image. |
| W-7 (Codex) | Medium | Public delivery reports contained raw build logs (could leak private source). | **Fixed**: the published report has results, hashes, sizes and counts only; full logs go to the encrypted engagement memory. |
| W-8 (Codex) | Medium | An overdue milestone was skipped and a later one worked out of order. | **Fixed**: the loop always stops at the first unfinished milestone. Found while testing: deadlines were compared with the box's clock; they now use the chain's time. |
| W-9 (Codex) | Low | A signed sealed-token upload could be replayed after rotation. | **Fixed**: a monotonic version is signed with each upload, and older versions are refused; the sealed context must match the contract. |
| I-1…I-7 (Codex) | Critical (design) | **Untrusted client builds cannot be isolated inside the box's container** (no namespaces or capabilities): the TEE socket (impersonation), cloud metadata (owner credentials), abstract sockets, local services, surviving processes; QEMU at the same uid is not a full boundary either. | **Decided jointly by Claude and Codex** (THIET-KE-NGHE-NHUNG.md §12): client code never runs in the AI box. The client's CI runs N1–N4; drawn reviewers reproduce disputed checks on disposable, identity-less builders. Until then long-term work stays **off** (fail closed, `isolation.js`). |
| CI-1 (Codex) | High | A check of the same name from another app or source could satisfy a required check. | **Fixed** (2026-11-21): re-runs count by their newest result; the same name from two sources is ambiguous and fails. Test added. |
| CI-2 (Codex) | High | The published CI report is the delivering box's statement, not independent proof. | **Accepted, documented**: it is the sealed box's reading of the client's CI (approved code, attested key) and is a necessary, not sufficient, condition in disputes; independent reproduction is §12 step 2. |
| CI-3 (Codex) | High | Concurrent steps could race on branches and state. | **Fixed**: one step at a time (single-flight), state written atomically (temp + rename). |
| CI-4 (Codex) | High | Pending state was cleared before the delivery transaction succeeded. | **Fixed**: the CI-passed state (commit, report) is kept until the delivery is on chain, then cleared. |
| CI-5 (Codex) | High | A dishonest client could make CI fail or stay silent, keep the pushed code and reclaim the escrow. | **Fixed by joint decision §13**: `Engagement.claimDone` lets the worker ask drawn reviewers once per milestone before the deadline; it freezes `reclaimLate`; the outcome is recorded in the worker's reputation. |
| CI-6 (Codex) | Medium | Only the first 100 CI results were read. | **Fixed**: every page is read (bounded), an incomplete read fails closed. |
| CI-7 (Codex) | Medium | Forge repository URLs were not canonicalised. | **Fixed**: the same strict allow-list check as the workspace; dot segments refused. |
| CI-8 (Codex) | Medium | The client can move the AI's branch after CI passed. | **Accepted**: the chain records the exact commit sha (content-addressed); the branch name is only a pointer. |
| CI-9 (Codex) | Low | `file://` repositories are allowed when `allowFileUrls` is on. | **Accepted**: test-only option, never set in the box (start.js does not pass it). |
| — (found in testing) | High | While a milestone was under dispute, the loop worked on the next milestone. | **Fixed**: a disputed milestone stops the loop like any unfinished one. |
| R-1 (Codex) | High | Reproduction: the git bundle carried the commit's whole history (unrelated proprietary code). | **Fixed** (2026-11-21): the worker sends a parentless snapshot of exactly the disputed tree plus the original commit object; the builder's supervisor hashes the commit object itself and checks the snapshot's tree against it. |
| R-2 (Codex) | High | The builder parsed the untrusted bundle with git as root. | **Fixed**: every git operation on the bundle runs as the sandbox user; the root supervisor only hashes bytes. |
| R-3 (Codex) | High | Result verification lacked bindings (tree, image, command records, `ok` consistency). | **Fixed**: all checked; tests for each. The production attestation verifier must authenticate the full canonical manifest (open: builder launcher + approved-image policy). |
| R-4 (Codex) | High | One verified failing reproduction decided the dispute automatically. | **Fixed (Claude agreed in part)**: a failing reproduction is evidence for the judgement; only a worker's claim over a failing client CI with no passing reproduction stays with the client. |
| R-5 (Codex) | Medium | Source delivery could repeat; bindings before disclosure. | **Fixed**: one bundle per job (remembered by the worker's box and refused twice by the service); commit and commands checked before sending. |
| R-6 (Codex) | High | No caps on commands, total time or request size. | **Fixed**: ≤ 10 commands of ≤ 300 characters, ≤ 1 h in total, request size refused before parsing; VM-level limits come with the launcher. |
| R-7 (Codex) | Medium | Anyone could fill a dispute's job slots; results without a source. | **Fixed**: jobs only for a live dispute on chain, one per builder key per dispute; results only after a source was sent, and only for that job; the source is deleted once the result is in. |
| L-1 (Codex) | High | Builder: the process exit did not stop the VM; failures exited 0; waits exceeded the VM lifetime. | **Fixed** (2026-11-21): failures write failed.json and exit 1; the launcher deletes the VM as soon as a result or a failure appears; source wait 90 min < 2 h VM life (deleted by the cloud anyway). |
| L-2 (Codex) | High | The build can reach the metadata server and so the job bucket. | **Mitigated by ordering, proven in the image**: job.json is read and source.sealed fetched before any client code runs; every sandbox process is killed before the signed result is written; a detached process trying to overwrite result.json fails. ### Slither results

**First run:** no High findings, and all 5 Medium findings were triaged. **Second run** (after F-1 and F-2): one High, `weak-prng` in the reviewer draw, see F-5. The new Medium findings are the same patterns: the `blockhash == 0` expiry check and the zero-initialised counter `got`.

Triage of the first run's Medium findings:

| Finding | Verdict |
|---|---|
| `minted == 0` strict equality in `AGTVault._mint` | False positive: it is a guard against zero-value deposits. |
| Locked ether in `AGTVault` | Intended: the Vault is permanent and has no withdraw function, as Charter Article 3 requires. |
| Uninitialised `last` in `AGTBridge.release` | Intended: the zero address is the start of the strictly increasing owner order. |
| Ignored return values of `latestRoundData` and `licenses()` | Safe: the fields used are checked (price > 0, freshness; active, agent, attestation). |

The 17 Low findings are external calls in loops (minting to known holders, licence look-ups) and timestamp comparisons (deadlines, day buckets). Neither is exploitable here.
