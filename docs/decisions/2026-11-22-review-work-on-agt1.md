### A-1 [High] Disputes can permanently strand escrow
**Where:** `chain/abci/app.js:804-808`, `445-467` · **Problem:** A dispute is assigned `reviewers: []` when no eligible same-trade, other-owner licence exists. The milestone remains disputed, but no reviewer can vote and settlement only happens after all reviewers vote. · **Scenario:** A contract milestone is disputed on a new or narrow-trade chain with no eligible reviewers; its escrow stays locked indefinitely. · **Fix:** Keep the dispute in a retryable “waiting for reviewers” state when the draw is empty, and define a deterministic fallback or timeout refund/settlement rule.

### A-2 [High] Price reports are not limited to one per owner
**Where:** `chain/abci/app.js:173-176`, `495-509` · **Problem:** Any active licensed AI can report, despite the stated one-voice-per-owner rule. Owners can hold multiple licences and therefore control multiple reports in the median. The ±10% step limit does not prevent a coordinated or repeated price walk. · **Scenario:** One owner obtains multiple licences and shifts a Kho asset’s reported price, changing how much AGT later deposits mint. · **Fix:** Select one eligible reporter per owner for both report acceptance and median calculation; consider a robust update rule that resists gradual price walking.

### A-3 [High] The proposer can grind reviewer draws
**Where:** `chain/abci/app.js:157-169`, `797-807` · **Problem:** Reviewer selection uses the finalized block hash, which the proposer can influence by varying proposal contents/order and potentially grinding proposals. The three-block delay does not help: the draw still uses a proposer-chosen block hash. · **Scenario:** A caretaker proposer searches for a block hash that selects favorable reviewers for a work submission or dispute. · **Fix:** Use an unpredictable, protocol-committed randomness source unavailable to the proposer when constructing the draw block, or a verifiable randomness mechanism.

### A-4 [High] Proposal validity depends on each validator’s external-chain view
**Where:** `chain/abci/app.js:719-740` · **Problem:** `ProcessProposal` accepts or rejects based on each node’s local BSC/Kho RPC results. Different RPC lag, outages, or reorg views can make honest validators disagree about the same proposal, harming liveness. Kho credits also keep issuance dependent on BSC and its RPCs. · **Scenario:** A proposer includes a deposit visible to some validators but not others; they reject the same proposal, or cannot make progress during an RPC outage. · **Fix:** Make external facts consensus-verifiable from committed proofs and a deterministic finality rule, or remove external-chain-dependent credits from proposal validity and issuance.

### A-5 [Medium] CheckTx mutates its shared state before validation completes
**Where:** `chain/abci/app.js:651-658`, `89-105` · **Problem:** `CheckTx` applies directly to `this.check`. Some handlers consume nonce, quota, or fee before later checks can throw; a rejected transaction therefore leaves mutations in the local CheckTx state. · **Scenario:** A malformed or unauthorized work transaction consumes the sender’s local free quota/nonce, causing subsequent valid transactions to fail CheckTx until the state is reset at commit. · **Fix:** Apply CheckTx transactions to a clone and replace `this.check` only after the entire transaction succeeds.

### A-6 [Medium] Contract reviewers can include an interested client
**Where:** `chain/abci/app.js:162-167`, `802-807` · **Problem:** Dispute draws exclude the worker’s holder but not the client. If the client holds a same-trade licence, its AI can be selected to judge its own dispute. · **Scenario:** A client who owns a licensed AI receives a reviewer vote in a dispute over its contract. · **Fix:** Exclude both client and worker owners from contract-dispute reviewer candidates.
