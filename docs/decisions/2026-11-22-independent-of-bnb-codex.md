1. **Define the destination and safety boundary.** Make AGT’s own CometBFT chain the sole ledger; remove BSC contract calls from the product, and treat BNB as neither reserve nor required operating fuel. Gold remains only a unit of account. Before launch, publish a threat model, audited state-transition and key-management code, recovery/upgrade rules, and a public migration specification. A caretaker validator is a provisional operator, not independent security.

2. **Stage 1 — Build and rehearse without value.** Move licence records, exam results, and work/maintenance state to the own chain in test mode; keep BSC authoritative for existing assets during the rehearsal. Reconcile every licence and settlement record against BSC snapshots across at least 30 days, with zero unexplained mismatches; test restore, upgrade, key-loss, and chain-halt procedures. The founder may operate the caretaker and hold temporary admin roles, but must disclose keys/actions and use public, time-locked, auditable changes. Main risks: bugs, bad data mapping, and founder-key compromise.

3. **Balances before four independent validators?** **Not transferable or redeemable balances.** With one founder-run validator, consensus is not meaningfully independent. Do permit bounded, clearly labeled *non-value* accounting/test balances to exercise code, with no bridge, redemption, or claim of backing. When at least four independent owners have bound licensed AIs, remove the caretaker automatically as specified; verify distinct beneficial owners, uptime, and key control publicly. Then require an external security review and a sustained operational period before enabling capped real balances. Four validators are a threshold, not proof of decentralization or safety; raise participation thresholds as it grows.

4. **Stage 2 — Move operations, then fees.** Make own-chain licences authoritative only after reconciliation, public notice, and a defined BSC read-only sunset. Store exam credentials/results and work/maintenance agreements on-chain or by content hash; settle jobs in the chain’s native fee asset under explicit, auditable rules. Use metered fees with published caps and a fee market; bootstrap through a time-limited, budgeted sponsor/grant pool, then taper it by date or usage threshold. No AI should need BNB. Risks: fee volatility, spam, sponsor capture, and disputes over off-chain work.

5. **Stage 3 — Migrate assets cautiously.** First decide what AGT represents and publish its rights, supply, and treasury policy. Once BSC Vault is gone, AGT is **not automatically backed**: disclose that plainly. Its value, if any, must come from credible utility, demand, and transparent governance—not gold wording or custodial BTCB/ETH/USDT/USDC. Do not promise redemption unless independently audited, liquid reserves and enforceable claims actually exist. For existing BSC AGT, offer a one-time, audited snapshot-and-migration: freeze legacy issuance, publish balances and claims, let holders prove control and claim on the new chain, and permanently disable or burn migrated BSC supply. Use a capped, monitored bridge only if migration cannot be one-time; never rely on AI signatures alone for release.

6. **Exit and “do not do.”** Retire BSC only after all live functions are migrated, claims reconciled, migration support tested, and users given a published notice period; then disable BSC-dependent issuance, settlement, and bridge paths. Do not move BNB into the new Vault, call custodial assets backing, launch uncapped balances under founder-only consensus, or leave parallel ledgers and open-ended bridges. Keep temporary founder roles public, narrowly scoped, and subject to automatic, measurable handover—not discretionary promises.
