1. **Ledger: port the whole value path, not a BSC wrapper.** Add these deterministic app transactions first:
   - `Transfer` and `KeyRotate` for AGT balances and licensed-AI key binding.
   - `LicenseApply`, `ExamSubmit`, and `ExamAttest/LicenseGrant` for applications, exam evidence, and issued/revoked licences.
   - `ContractCreate/Accept/Close` for client terms and `WorkSubmit/WorkSettle` for evidence-backed payment and any work reward.
   
   Record contract terms, exam/version hashes, attestations, settlement amounts, and fee recipients in chain state/events. Make settlement atomic: validate the contract and work evidence, debit the payer, pay the signing AI owners’ fixed fee share, and mint any permitted reward. No BSC bridge or off-chain “balance” should be authoritative.

   **Day-one licensing:** no founder-only approvals. Put 5 independent bootstrap examiners in genesis, with public keys, criteria, and a 3-of-5 grant threshold; exclude the founder from holding a decisive share. Applications and scored evidence are public, with a challenge window. Once the existing independent-owner count reaches 4, automatically remove the caretaker role and hand licensing to the on-chain owner/evaluator threshold. Publish the transition rule and examiner conflicts now; do not sell a founder’s discretionary approval as decentralization.

2. **Backing and issuance: call it work-funded inflation, not BNB-backed.** AGT on the own chain comes only from a valid `WorkSettle`; there is no Vault or other mint path. Require genesis to contain an immutable `work_mint_cap`, per-epoch budget, and reward schedule. The cap must be a separately disclosed number—not the old Vault limit presented as backing. If no defensible number is approved today, set the cap to zero until a public on-chain parameter vote sets it. Enforce the lifetime cap and epoch budget in app code; settlement proofs must meet published criteria and be attested by independent licensed keys. Neither caretaker nor founder gets a mint/admin transaction. Any later parameter change uses the published participation-based threshold and becomes effective automatically after its timelock.

3. **Fees and spam:** make only `LicenseApply` and the first `ExamSubmit` per applicant key fee-free, each once, with per-key and per-block limits. Require AGT for transfers, contracts, repeated exams, and settlements. For other onboarding actions, fund a finite genesis sponsor pool; cap subsidy per licence and per epoch, debit it on-chain, and reject when exhausted. Do not issue a freely transferable “free gas” balance.

4. **Dependencies you cannot remove today:** TEE vendors can mis-attest or discontinue service—support multiple vendors and publish measurements. Cloud can fail or censor—make validators deployable elsewhere and publish snapshots. Model providers can change outputs or shut off access—pin model/version hashes where possible, log provider/version, and support alternatives. The founder’s laptop is a launch dependency—publish reproducible builds, genesis, keys/roles, and bootstrap instructions; get independent operators online before claiming independence.

5. **BSC contracts:** keep the legacy token transferable, but halt new minting and Vault-dependent work issuance; disable privileged mint/admin paths where safely possible. Publish the exact snapshot block and state plainly that it is not a bridge, redemption promise, or continuing backing. Don’t freeze ordinary holders’ transfers without a concrete safety reason.
