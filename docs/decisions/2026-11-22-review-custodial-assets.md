### V-1 [High] Deposits can bypass classification by arriving first
**Where:** `AGTVault.sol:95-100, 129-140` · **Problem:** `setCustodial` adds past mints to the group total but never checks whether those past deposits already exceed either limit. Before classification, deposits skip both checks. **Scenario:** The owner accepts a custodial token, deposits it while it is unmarked, then marks it; the Vault retains an over-limit custodial allocation. **Fix:** At minimum, enforce both limits when marking and reject a classification that would leave the Vault over-limit. This cannot prevent the owner from simply never marking the token; the policy therefore depends on trustworthy, timely classification.

### V-2 [High] Custodian groups can be split to evade the group limit
**Where:** `AGTVault.sol:95-100, 136-140` · **Problem:** The owner chooses any nonzero group ID, and there is no enforcement that tokens controlled by the same custodian share one ID. The 10% cap is therefore only per chosen ID, not per actual custodian. **Scenario:** Mark two tokens from the same custodian under separate IDs and deposit up to 10% of AGT minted into each. **Fix:** Use a canonical, auditable custodian identifier and require all that custodian’s tokens to use it; document that the guarantee still relies on correct classification.

**Valuation:** Deposit-time AGT amounts are acceptable if the intended limit is historical issuance concentration and avoiding an enforcement-time oracle is the priority. They do **not** cap current marked-to-market custodial exposure: a depeg or price change can make the collateral’s present value share differ substantially from its share of AGT minted. `mintedTotal` otherwise tracks successful Vault mints; an AGT mint-cap revert rolls back the deposit and these accounting updates. Exact-limit rounding is handled correctly by the strict `>` checks.
